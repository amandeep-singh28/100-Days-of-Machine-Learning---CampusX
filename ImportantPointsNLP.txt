- split() and word_tokenize() works differently. split() ignores punctuation, etc etc
So, in NLP you must use word_tokenize() to get the best results

- word_tokenize is used for word splitting including punctuation too
While sent_tokenize does the splitting when a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark arrives
For example:
paragraph = "I love NLP. It is amazing. AI is powerful, smart. Hello! Amandeep? Singh"
Output:-['I love NLP.', 'It is amazing.', 'AI is powerful, smart.', 'Hello!', 'Amandeep?', 'Singh']
