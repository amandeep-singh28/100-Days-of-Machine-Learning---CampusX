What does NLP stand for?
Natural Language Processing

It is a field of AI that helps computers understand human language.

Human language examples:

English
Hindi
Punjabi
French

Computers understand:

Numbers
Vectors
Matrices

NLP is the bridge between them.

For example:-
Suppose I have written "I LOVE PIZZA". We, as humans, can already understand what this sentence means, like what love is and what pizza is. But for a machine, it doesn't understand whether the sentence is positive or negative. It just sees the text as characters.
