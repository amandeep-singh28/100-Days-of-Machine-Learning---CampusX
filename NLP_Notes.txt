What does NLP stand for?
Natural Language Processing

It is a field of AI that helps computers understand human language.

Human language examples:

English
Hindi
Punjabi
French

Computers understand:

Numbers
Vectors
Matrices

NLP is the bridge between them.

For example:-
Suppose I have written "I LOVE PIZZA". We, as humans, can already understand what this sentence means, like what love is and what pizza is. But for a machine, it doesn't understand whether the sentence is positive or negative. It just sees the text as characters.

-> Applications of NLP
(i)Sentiment Analysis
Determining whether a text is of positive, negative or neutral sentiment.
Used in:
    Product Reviews

(ii)Machine Translation
Translating text from one language to another
For example:-
    Hindi -> English
    Hindi -> Punjabi
Used in:
    Google Translation

(iii)Chatbots and Virtual Assistants
Understanding user queries and giving them correct answer
Used in:
    ChatGPT, SIRI, Alexa


(iv)Spam Detection
Classifying emails as spam or ham.

-> Working of NLP

Text
 ↓
Cleaning
 ↓
Tokenization
 ↓
Vectorization
 ↓
Machine Learning Model
 ↓
Prediction

For example:
"I Love Cricket"

Firtly it will do the preprocessing, for example:- Converting the text into lowercase, removing extra spaces, etc etc.

Than it will do Tokenization, sentence is broken down into smaller units called tokens(Like a STRING of words)
["i", "love", "cricket"]

Than, Vectorization, it means that converting text into numbers because ML models work upon numerical data
[0.2, 0.7, 0.5]

Than, Apply an NLP model and then Result.

-> Important NLP terms
(i)Corpus
Collection of documents
Example:
Doc1: I love cricket
Doc2: NLP is interesting
Doc3: Machine learning is powerful

Together = Corpus

(ii)Document
Single text file or Document

(iii)Vocabulary
Unique words present in Corpus
Example:
I love cricket
I love NLP

Vocabulary:
I
love
cricket
NLP

Size = 4
